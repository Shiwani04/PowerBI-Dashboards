# 🛍️ Retail Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 About the Project
An end-to-end Power BI dashboard analyzing 4 years of retail sales data across
products, regions, and customers — built to answer a real business question:
is sales growth actually translating into profit, or are some parts of the
business quietly underperforming?

The project covers the full analytics workflow: data cleaning, star schema
data modeling, DAX measure development, time intelligence, and a 4-page
interactive report.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
Sales grew 20.36% in the most recent year alone — but is that growth
translating into equally strong profit, or are certain categories, regions,
and customers dragging performance down without anyone noticing?

## 🔍 Key Insights

- 🪑 **Furniture sells well but barely profits** — 32.3% of total sales, but
  only 6.44% of total profit (2.7% margin vs 17–18% for other categories)

- 🌍 **Regional performance isn't just about sales volume** — West leads in
  sales (₹0.76M), but East has the best profit margin (15.46%). South is the
  weakest performer on both fronts (₹0.40M sales, 7.51% margin)

- 👤 **The #1 customer by revenue is losing the business money** — Sean Miller
  generated ₹25,043 in sales but at a **-8% profit margin**

- 🧩 **Customer revenue is healthily diversified** — top 10 customers make up
  only 6.69% of total revenue, no risky dependency on a few big accounts

- 📉 **Profit growth is starting to lag behind sales growth** — in 2017, sales
  grew 20.36% while profit grew only 14.24%, an early signal worth monitoring

---

## 💡 Business Impact
- Investigate Furniture pricing/cost structure before expanding the category
- Study East region's practices to help improve South's margins
- Review Sean Miller's account terms (discounting, fulfillment cost)
- Monitor future margins closely — sales outpacing profit is an early warning sign
